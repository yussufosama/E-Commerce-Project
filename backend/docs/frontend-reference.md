# Triple-Seven frontend reference

The owner's WhatsApp video supplied on September 28 is the intended design reference. The Lovable marketing homepage is not the reference.

Observed desktop homepage:
- Almost-black background, pale cream text, thin divider lines.
- Compact Triple Seven wordmark at top left; Collection, Manifesto and No. 777 navigation at right.
- Wide fashion photograph hero with oversized condensed uppercase "ROLL FOR IT" text at lower left.
- Small introductory copy and light rectangular collection button below the hero.
- Repeating Triple Seven / 777 / Make Your Own Luck text strip.
- "DROP 01 / GENESIS" collection heading and four image-collage product cards.
- Visible products: Bone Crewneck, Void Crewneck, Probability Hoodie, Seven Blood Hoodie.
- Cream manifesto panel with a Triple Seven 777 cube logo at left and "WE DON'T BET ON CHANCE. WE BET ON THE GRIND." at right.
- Dark newsletter/footer area with "HEAR ABOUT THE NEXT ROLL" heading and a minimal email field.

The video shows homepage scrolling and closer views. Product-detail, cart, checkout and mobile layouts are not shown. Original logo and clothing photographs have not been supplied separately; the recording contains perspective distortion, screen reflections and the surrounding room. Use original assets for an accurate final recreation rather than presenting cropped monitor footage as product photography.

Implementation target remains React connected to the existing Node/Mongoose backend. Do not invent inventory, product prices or newsletter subscriptions from the visual reference.
